## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
DayKit is a toolkit for your day. One app holding the tools you reach for anyway — your alarm, your timers, the weather, the washing machine — with the parts you don't need tucked out of the way.

What ties them together is that they respond to your day instead of sitting there waiting for input. Your alarm reads your calendar. Your weather warns you about the appointment you're heading to. And if you run Home Assistant, most of these parts can reach into your house.


ALARMS THAT FOLLOW YOUR CALENDAR

Set an alarm once and stop maintaining it. DayKit reads the calendars already on your phone and schedules your alarm around what's actually planned.

• Pick which calendars count and how much time you need before an appointment
• The meeting moves an hour later, your alarm moves with it
• Write your own rules per calendar or per type of appointment
• See every upcoming alarm in one list, so nothing catches you out
• Full-screen wake-up on your lock screen, with snooze
• Survives a reboot


TIMERS AND STOPWATCH

• Run several timers at once, each with its own name
• Set the sound, volume and vibration per timer
• Save a timer and restart it later with one tap
• Stopwatch with a lock screen notification


APPLIANCE PLANNER

• Enter how long a wash or dishwasher cycle runs and see when it finishes
• Or work backwards: say when you want it done and DayKit calculates the delay start
• Save your programmes under your own names instead of retyping them


WEATHER AND WEATHER ALERTS

• Current forecast for where you are
• A daily weather notification at a time you choose
• A warning when bad weather lands on a calendar appointment, so you leave earlier or take a coat
• An alert when the temperature swings sharply


CALCULATOR

A straightforward calculator, one tap away.


HOME ASSISTANT READY

If you run Home Assistant, the parts of DayKit stop being just an app:

• Pair with a QR code or setup code
• Run scripts when an alarm fires or a timer ends — fade the lights up, open the curtains
• Presence detection, so an alarm only sounds when you're home
• Out-of-bed check through a sensor: the alarm stops when you're actually up, not when you reach for it
• Play your wake-up sound through a speaker in the house instead of your phone

All optional. DayKit works fine without it.


BUILD YOUR OWN KIT

• Add your own alarm sounds
• Change backgrounds, colours and the clock layout
• Choose which parts appear and in what order
• Put shortcuts to individual tools on your home screen
• Back up and restore everything


PRIVACY

No account, no ads. Your calendar data stays on your phone. Your location is used only to fetch the forecast.


18 LANGUAGES

English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.


DayKit is under active development. Something missing or not working the way you'd expect? Let us know — feedback gets read.
```
